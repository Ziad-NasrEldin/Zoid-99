# Zoid 99 Agent Guidance

## Product context

Read `CONTEXT.md` before planning or implementing product changes.

## Issue tracker

Product requirements live in `.scratch/<feature-slug>/PRD.md`.
Implementation issues live in `.scratch/<feature-slug>/issues/`.
Use `ready-for-agent`, `needs-triage`, `needs-info`, `ready-for-human`, and `wontfix` as local status values.

## Working rules

- Keep the MVP focused on research collection, research interpretation, and timely notifications.
- Do not add content generation, publishing workflows, content calendars, or creator-performance analytics unless a later PRD explicitly adds them.
- Prefer official APIs, feeds, and platform-supported integrations.
- Never present an unverified claim as confirmed.
- Keep source links and timestamps attached to every research opportunity.
