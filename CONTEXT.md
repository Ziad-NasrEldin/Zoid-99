# Zoid 99 Product Context

## Purpose

Zoid 99 is a native SwiftUI macOS research intelligence application for an Arabic AI video creator.
It monitors global AI developments, US creators, Arabic audience demand, and six research sources in one place.
It identifies timely, verified opportunities that can become Arabic YouTube videos, Shorts, Instagram Reels, or posts.

## Domain glossary

### Source Item

A video, post, trend, comment, release, article, or research update collected from one connected source.

### Story Cluster

Source Items that describe the same underlying development.

### Opportunity

A Story Cluster that has been verified and scored for freshness, momentum, Arabic coverage gap, and relevance to Egypt or the Gulf.

### Watchlist

The creators, official sources, companies, keywords, topics, countries, and languages that the user wants Zoid 99 to monitor.

### Research Brief

The factual summary, original sources, timeline, creator coverage, regional relevance, and uncertainty attached to an Opportunity.
It does not contain a generated script.

## MVP boundaries

- Zoid 99 targets macOS and uses SwiftUI.
- Zoid 99 monitors YouTube, Google Trends, Instagram, comments, US and official sources, and X.
- Zoid 99 provides Today, Live Radar, Topics, Comments, Watchlists, Notifications, Sources and Settings, and Opportunity Detail.
- Zoid 99 does not generate scripts or publishing assets.
- Zoid 99 does not include a content calendar or production workflow.
- Zoid 99 does not analyze the performance of the user's published content.
- Zoid 99 does not publish or reply automatically.
